✨ What are the auditions like?

They're a freestyle audition. There is no audition script, so performers can show their own creativity.

👑 What are we auditioning for?

We're auditioning for SIX, the very first production presented by The Stargazing Theater.
QUESTION 04

🎬 What happens after auditions?

After auditions, we'll work toward cast announcements, rehearsals, and production preparation.

About The Theater

A place where stories come to life.
